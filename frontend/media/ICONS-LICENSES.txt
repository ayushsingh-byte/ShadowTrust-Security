The "Built on" row: marks of the projects the platform runs on
==============================================================

The marks are shown only to say which projects ShadowTrust is built on. The names and logos
belong to their owners. No partnership or endorsement is implied.

Cowrie, Dionaea and Honeytrap publish no logo in their repositories. ClamAV and MobSF publish
colour pictures that do not work in a one-colour row. Those five are set in type.

Simple Icons 16.34.0, https://simpleicons.org (docker, fastapi, mariadb, python, nginx,
kalilinux, apacheguacamole, leaflet, openstreetmap, chartdotjs)

Simple Icons as a collection is released under CC0 1.0 Universal
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Its disclaimer says this does not make
every mark in it CC0, and asks users to follow each owner's rules:
https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/DISCLAIMER.md

What the package records per mark: Apache Guacamole is Apache-2.0
(https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Logo rules are linked for MariaDB
(https://mariadb.com/about-us/logos/), Python (https://www.python.org/community/logos/),
NGINX (https://www.nginx.com/press/), Kali Linux (https://www.kali.org/docs/policy/trademark/)
and OpenStreetMap (https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Trademark_Policy).

Owners' rules as read on 2026-10-07. Check them again before this page is published or used
commercially; on a private deployment nothing is published:
- Docker (https://www.docker.com/legal/trademark-guidelines/): the name may be used to refer
  to the product, the whale logo needs Docker's authorisation.
- OpenStreetMap: the plain name may be used to refer to the project; the logo may not be
  modified, and uses outside section 3 of the policy need a licence.
- MariaDB: asks that the colour of its logo is not changed.
- Kali Linux: asks that its marks are not modified and that a trademark statement is shown.
The rules for FastAPI, Python, NGINX, Leaflet, Chart.js and Apache Guacamole were not read.

To drop a mark and keep the name, remove the <svg> from its two list items in index.html.
